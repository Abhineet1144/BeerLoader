BEER LOADER - SESSION 12 SUMMARY (2026-10-02)
==============================================

MAJOR FINDING: The "zeros at 0x1423b1403" were caused by the loader itself,
not by the game. The generic sync allocator used MAP_FIXED|MAP_ANONYMOUS
without checking whether faultaddr was inside the guest image. When a fault
occurred with a stale register pointing into .text, it zeroed the code page
and removed execute permission. GDB watchpoint confirmed the bytes at
0x1423b1403 never actually change (stay 48 8b c8 ff 15...).
